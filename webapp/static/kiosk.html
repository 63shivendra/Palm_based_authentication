Palm Kiosk

Register a palm

Show your open palm, follow the finger prompts, and get a palm ID. The same palm cannot be registered twice.

Pay with palm

No phone, no card: show your registered palm and follow the prompts. The kiosk finds who you are.

Photos are processed in memory and never saved. Stored per person: encrypted palm templates and a 512-byte cancellable search template. Liveness: random finger counts checked on the server.
