My account

Show your palm to see your balance and recent payments, or to erase your palm data.

Photos are processed in memory and never saved. Every check (photo quality, liveness, matching) runs on the kiosk server. Payments here are a simulation: nothing is sent to a bank, NPCI or UPI.

Register your palm

Consent (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023)
  • Purpose: identify you for payments at this kiosk.
  • Stored: an encrypted palm template and a 512-byte cancellable search code, on this computer only. Your photos are not stored.
  • Not stored: images of your hand. The template is a set of encrypted palm-line features, not a picture; it is decrypted only in memory while matching.
  • You can erase everything at any time under "My account", or ask the admin.
